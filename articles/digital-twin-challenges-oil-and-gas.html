Digital Twin challenges in oil & gas: 10 problems and how to solve them

By Neeraj Sonagara·Digital Twin & Digital Transformation Consultant·6 min read

Almost every oil & gas Digital Twin program meets the same ten problems. Very few of them are about the software. Knowing them early is the cheapest way to avoid them.

10 problemsAlmost none are about the software
Data firstQuality, ownership and standards
Fix in orderStart with the foundation

1. Engineering data scattered across systems

Drawings in an EDMS, equipment in a CMMS, costs in ERP, readings in a historian. Nobody sees the whole asset. Fix: one governed source of asset information, with the other systems reading from it instead of keeping copies.

2. Inconsistent tags and equipment records

The same pump has three names in three systems. Fix: a tag and equipment standard, data quality rules, and alignment to ISO 15926, ISO 14224 and CFIHOS.

3. Poor handover from EPC to operations

Facilities start up with incomplete or unusable engineering data. Fix: define data requirements in the contract and validate deliverables before acceptance. See the EPC handover guide.

4. Documents not linked to assets

People search folders instead of asking about an asset. Fix: link every document to its tags and equipment, and model the relationships, often with a knowledge graph.

5. IT and OT that do not talk

SCADA, DCS and historians sit apart from CMMS and engineering systems. Fix: a governed integration layer and a shared asset hierarchy. More in IT/OT convergence in oil & gas.

6. Legacy systems and risky migration

Old platforms hold years of valuable but messy data. Fix: staged migration with automated quality gates, not a one-off copy.

7. Nobody owns the data

Quality decays the day a project ends. Fix: named data owners, clear rules, and service agreements that keep quality accountable.

8. Pilots that never scale

A pilot works on hand-cleaned data and then stalls. Fix: design the pilot to be repeatable. See why pilots never scale.

9. An unclear business case

A Digital Twin that cannot name its value gets cut. Fix: start from use cases such as faster information retrieval, turnaround planning or maintenance, and measure them.

10. Low user adoption

If people do not use it, it is not a Digital Twin. Fix: embed it in daily workflows, train users, and give them a support route.

Where to start: assess how ready your information is before choosing or expanding a platform. The weakest of these ten sets your ceiling.
