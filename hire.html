FAQ

Questions people ask before hiring

What does a Digital Twin consultant do?

A Digital Twin consultant helps an organisation plan, design and deliver a governed, connected digital representation of its physical assets. In my work that means building the information foundation (engineering data, documents, 3D models and operational data), designing the architecture that connects it, and selecting and rolling out the platform.

Are you available for freelance or contract work?

Yes. I take on freelance projects, longer contract roles and advisory engagements, remote or on-site. Email or WhatsApp me a short brief of the problem and I will reply with how I can help.

How much experience do you have?

More than 18 years: a refinery mechanical engineer at Reliance Industries from 2008, then information management and Digital Transformation lead on Chevron programs via L&T Technology Services (2012–2025), and now Digital Twin Senior Consultant at EY GDS, contracted to ADNOC. I am a TOGAF® certified Enterprise Architect.

Which industries and technologies do you work with?

Mainly oil & gas and other asset-heavy industries. Platforms include AVEVA AIM / NET, Kognitwin, Cognite Data Fusion and Azure Digital Twins, plus Neo4j, SQL and ETL, Power BI, OSI PI, SAP, Maximo and Meridium, and standards such as ISO 15926, ISO 14224, CFIHOS and DEXPI.

Where do you work?

I am based in India with active engagements across the UAE and the USA. I work remotely with clients worldwide and on-site by arrangement.

Can you help with Digital Transformation, not only Digital Twin?

Yes. I lead Digital Transformation programs across engineering, operations and IT: target architecture, data governance, IT/OT integration, stakeholder alignment and value-based prioritisation of use cases.
